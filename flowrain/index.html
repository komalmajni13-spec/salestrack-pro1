Enter Leaves Taken This Month

Har employee ki monthly leave allowance 1.5 din hai. Jo leave bachegi woh next month carry forward ho jayegi. Jo extra leave li hai uski salary se deduction calculate ho jayega. Attendance (check-in/check-out ya Manual Attendance) se "Leave Taken" hamesha automatic calculate hota hai. Yahan number edit karke "Save & Calculate" dabana sirf tab tak rahega jab tak us employee ki agli attendance entry na aa jaye — uske baad wapas attendance ke hisaab se update ho jayega.
